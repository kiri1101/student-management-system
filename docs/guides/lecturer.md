# Lecturer Guide

A plain-language guide to using SchuLyf as a lecturer — from signing in to recording exam marks.

---

## Who this guide is for

You are a **lecturer** (teaching staff). SchuLyf is where you plan each course you teach, schedule and run class sessions, take attendance, set and grade assignments, and record continuous-assessment (CA) and exam marks. This guide walks you through every task you can do, what you will see on screen, and what happens after each click.

A few terms used throughout:

- **Course plan** — your written description of a course. You submit it for approval before you can do anything else with that course.
- **Student Affairs Officer (SAO)** — the staff member who approves your course plans and publishes your students' results. You will see them referred to by this name on some screens.
- **Cohort** — the group of students taking a course (a year group at a given level). You do not enrol students yourself; the system already knows who is in each course.
- **CA** — *continuous assessment*: the coursework mark a student earns during the term, separate from the final exam mark.
- **Matricule** — a student's unique registration number, shown next to each student's name.

## Getting in

### Your account

You do **not** sign yourself up. An administrator creates your account and emails you an **invitation**. That email contains a one-time link to set your password. Open the link, choose a password, and your account is ready.

If you ever lose access, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page to set a new password.

### Signing in

1. Go to the SchuLyf sign-in page. The heading reads **Welcome back**.
2. In the **Username** field, enter any one of: your email address, your **employee ID** (for example `EMP-001`), or — if you also have one — your matricule. (Hover the small info icon beside the label to see this reminder.)
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Tick **Remember me** if you are on your own device.
5. Click **Log in**.

You land on your **Lecturer Dashboard**.

> Your account must have a verified email to reach the lecturer pages. If you set your password through the invitation link, this is already handled. If you change your email later (see **Settings**), you may be asked to verify the new address before some pages are available.

---

## What you can do

After signing in you see the **Lecturer Dashboard**:

- A welcome banner ("Welcome back").
- A **My courses** button (top right) that takes you to your course list.
- A **My profile** card showing your **Department**, **Specialization**, and **Hired on** date.

From there, everything happens inside **My courses**. For each course you teach you can:

- Write and submit its **course plan** for approval.
- Schedule **sessions** and mark **attendance** (once the plan is approved).
- Create **assignments** and grade student **submissions** (once the plan is approved).
- Enter **CA and exam results** (once the plan is approved).

> **The golden rule:** a course's plan must be **approved** before its Sessions, Assignments, and Results become available. Until then, those buttons are greyed out with a tooltip explaining why.

If the dashboard shows *"No lecturer profile found"*, contact an administrator — your account has not been fully set up as a lecturer.

---

## Your courses at a glance

Click **My courses** (or the **Courses** breadcrumb). You see a table of every course assigned to you. Each row shows:

- **Course** — the title and its code.
- **Level**, **Year**, **Sem.** (semester), and **Credits**.
- **Plan** — a coloured tag showing the plan's state: **Draft**, **Submitted**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**. If a plan was rejected, the SAO's review note appears in red right under the tag.
- Action buttons: **Sessions**, **Assignments**, **Results**, and **Edit plan**.

The **Sessions**, **Assignments**, and **Results** buttons only work once the plan is **Approved**. Before that they are disabled; hovering shows *"Approve the course plan to manage …"*. **Edit plan** is always available.

If the table says *"You have no assigned courses,"* no courses have been assigned to you yet — the SAO assigns lecturers to courses.

## Task walkthroughs

### 1. Write and submit a course plan

A course plan must be approved before you can schedule sessions, set assignments, or record marks. New courses start as a **Draft**.

1. On **My courses**, find the course and click **Edit plan**.
2. You land on the **Course plan** page. It shows the course code and title, a status tag, and the course's **Level / Year / Semester / Credits**.
3. In the **Description** box, type your plan for the course.
4. Click **Save plan** to keep your work without submitting. You can return and edit it again.
5. When you are ready for review, click **Submit for approval**.

**How you can tell it worked:** the status tag changes to **Submitted**, and the plan is now with the SAO. You cannot edit a submitted plan further until a decision is made.

**If your plan is rejected:** the status tag becomes **Rejected** and a red **Review notes** message shows what the SAO wants changed. Edit the **Description** to address the notes, then click **Submit for approval** again.

**If the Submit button is greyed out:** only a **Draft** or **Rejected** plan can be submitted. A plan that is already **Submitted** or **Approved** cannot be resubmitted — a small note under the button explains this. An approved plan is final and is not re-reviewed.

---

### 2. Schedule and manage class sessions

A **session** is one scheduled class meeting. Sessions live under an **approved** course.

**Open the Sessions page**

1. On **My courses**, click **Sessions** on an approved course.
2. You see the **Sessions** page with a table of existing sessions (Scheduled time, Topic, Duration, Status) and an **Add session** button.

> If the plan is not yet approved, a yellow message reads *"The course plan must be approved before you can schedule sessions or mark attendance,"* and **Add session** is disabled.

**Add a session**

1. Click **Add session**. A dialog opens.
2. Set **Scheduled for** — pick the date and time.
3. Enter the **Topic**.
4. Set **Duration (minutes)** (defaults to 60).
5. Click **Add session**.

The dialog closes and the new session appears in the table with status **Scheduled**.

**Reschedule a session (and notify your students)**

1. In the session's row, click **Edit**. A dialog opens with the current details.
2. Change **Scheduled for**, **Topic**, or **Duration**.
3. A note reminds you: *"Students in this cohort will be emailed and notified in-app when you reschedule."*
4. Click **Save changes**.

**What happens:** if you moved the session to a different (future) time, every student in the cohort is automatically emailed and gets an in-app alert. If you only changed the topic or duration, students are **not** notified — that keeps minor edits quiet.

**Cancel a session (lecturer absence / class called off)**

1. In the session's row, click **Cancel**.
2. A dialog asks you to confirm. Enter a **Reason (shared with students)** — this text is shown to them.
3. Click **Cancel session** to confirm, or **Keep session** to back out.

**What happens:** the session's status becomes **Cancelled**, students see it marked cancelled, and — for a future session — every student in the cohort is **emailed and notified in-app** with your reason. This is how you tell a class you will be absent. The **Cancel** button is disabled for a session that is already cancelled.

> Sessions are never deleted — cancelling keeps the record so there is a clear, trusted history.

---

### 3. Mark attendance for a session

1. Open the **Sessions** page for the course.
2. In the session's row, click **Attendance**.
3. The **Attendance** page lists every student in the cohort, each with their name and **matricule**, and a dropdown to set their attendance.
4. For each student choose **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused**. (Everyone starts at **Present** so you only change the exceptions.)
5. Click **Save attendance**.

**How you can tell it worked:** a confirmation appears and your choices are saved. You can reopen the page and adjust marks later — saving again simply updates them.

**If the list is empty** (*"No students are enrolled in this course cohort yet"*): no active students currently match this course's level and year. New enrolments will appear automatically.

---

### 4. Create assignments and grade submissions

Assignments live under an **approved** course.

**Open the Assignments page**

1. On **My courses**, click **Assignments** on an approved course.
2. You see the **Assignments** table (Title, Due, Max score, Submissions) and a **New assignment** button.

> If the plan is not yet approved, a yellow message says the plan must be approved first, and **New assignment** is disabled.

**Create an assignment**

1. Click **New assignment**. A dialog opens.
2. Enter a **Title**.
3. Enter **Instructions** for students.
4. Set **Due at** (date and time).
5. Set **Max score** (defaults to 20).
6. Click **Create assignment**.

The dialog closes and the assignment appears in the table.

**Edit or delete an assignment**

- Click **Edit** on a row to change the title, instructions, due date, or max score, then **Save changes**.
- Click **Delete** to remove it. You are asked to confirm with **Delete assignment**; note that **all student submissions for it will be removed**, so delete with care. Choose **Keep assignment** to back out.

**Review and grade submissions**

1. In the assignment's row, the **Submissions** column shows how many students have submitted and how many you have graded — for example *"12 (5 graded)"*.
2. Click **Submissions**. You land on the **Submissions** page listing each student's name, matricule, submission time (with a **Late** tag if it was past the due time), score, and status.
3. To open a student's file, click **View**. A viewer opens — PDFs and images display in place; you can also **Open in new tab** or **Download**.
4. To grade, click **Grade** on the row. A dialog opens.
5. Enter the **Score (out of [max score])** and optional **Feedback**.
6. Click **Save grade**.

**How you can tell it worked:** the status tag changes to **Graded** and the score appears in the **Score** column. The student can see your score and feedback once it's saved. You can re-open **Grade** to revise it.

> The score cannot exceed the assignment's max score — the field won't let you enter a higher number.

---

### 5. Enter CA and exam results

Results live under an **approved** course. You enter the marks; the **SAO publishes them** to students.

1. On **My courses**, click **Results** on an approved course.
2. The **Results** page lists every student in the course (name + matricule) with columns for **CA**, **Exam**, **Final**, **Grade**, and **Status**.
3. A blue note explains: *"Enter CA and exam scores, then save. Fully-scored results are published to students by the Student Affairs Officer. Published rows are locked."*
4. Type each student's **CA** score and **Exam** score (whole numbers, 0–100).
5. Click **Save results**.

**What happens:**

- Once **both** a CA and an exam score are present for a student, the **Final** score and a letter **Grade** are calculated automatically.
- Saved marks stay in **Draft** status — students cannot see them yet.
- The summary line at the top shows counts: how many are *draft*, how many *published*, and how many are *ready to publish*.

**Publishing:** you do **not** publish results yourself. The SAO publishes the fully-scored rows, and students see them only after that. Once a row is **Published** it is **locked** — its CA and Exam fields are greyed out and you can no longer change them.

**If the list is empty** (*"This course has no enrolled students yet"*): no active students currently match the course's level and year.

> If a student later challenges a published mark, they can raise a dispute. Disputes are reviewed by the SAO/administration — there is no dispute screen on your side.

---

### 6. Update your settings

Open **Settings** from the app menu. You will find:

- **Profile** — update your **Name**, **Email address**, and optional **Phone number**, then **Save**. If you change your email you may be prompted to verify the new address; use the **resend the verification email** link if needed.
- **[Lecturer] profile** (the staff-profile tab) — set your **Department**, **Specialization**, and **Hired on** date, then **Save**. (Your name and email stay on the **Profile** tab.) These details appear on your dashboard's **My profile** card.
- **Security** — change your password under **Update password** (enter your current password, then your new one twice, and **Save password**). If your account allows it, you can also set up **Two-factor authentication** for an extra sign-in step.
- **Appearance** — switch the app's light/dark look.

## Notifications

SchuLyf keeps your students informed automatically so you don't have to chase them:

- **When you cancel a future session**, every student in the cohort is **emailed** and gets an **in-app alert** carrying the reason you entered. This is the trusted way to announce an absence.
- **When you reschedule a session to a new time**, the cohort is likewise emailed and notified in-app. (Changing only the topic or duration sends nothing.)

You don't need to do anything extra — these go out the moment you save the cancel or reschedule.

As a lecturer you don't have an in-app notification inbox of your own; the in-app feed is on the student side. Decisions that affect your own account — for example a course plan being approved or rejected — show up as the **Plan** status changing in **My courses** (with the SAO's review note if it was rejected).

## FAQ / troubleshooting

**The Sessions / Assignments / Results buttons are greyed out.**
The course plan isn't approved yet. Open **Edit plan**, write the plan, and click **Submit for approval**. Once the SAO approves it, those buttons become active.

**My plan was rejected — what now?**
Open **Edit plan**. Read the red **Review notes**, update the **Description** to address them, and click **Submit for approval** again.

**I can't submit my plan — the button is disabled.**
Only **Draft** or **Rejected** plans can be submitted. If it already says **Submitted**, it's awaiting the SAO's decision. If it says **Approved**, it's final and doesn't need resubmitting.

**A student says they didn't know class was cancelled.**
Use the **Cancel** button on the session and enter a reason. For a future session, that automatically emails and in-app notifies the whole cohort. Word of mouth isn't needed.

**The attendance / results list is empty.**
No active students currently match this course's level and academic year. Students appear automatically once enrolled at the matching level and year — there is no manual enrolment step on your side.

**I want to fix a mark I already entered.**
You can edit any **Draft** result and save again. Once a result is **Published** by the SAO it's locked and can't be changed from your screen.

**I graded a submission incorrectly.**
Re-open **Submissions**, click **Grade** on that student's row, change the score or feedback, and **Save grade**.

**I can't open a student's submitted file.**
Use the **View** button in the **File** column. If it won't display in the viewer, use **Download** from the viewer footer to open it on your device.

**I changed my email and now some pages won't load.**
A changed email may need re-verifying. On the **Profile** settings page, use the **resend the verification email** link, then click the link in that email.
