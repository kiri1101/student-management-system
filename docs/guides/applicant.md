# Applicant Guide

*SchuLyf — Student Management System*

---

## Who this guide is for

You are a **prospective student** applying for admission. This guide walks you through creating an account, submitting an application for a programme, tracking its progress, and what happens once you are admitted. No technical knowledge is needed — every step describes exactly what you see on screen and what to click.

A few terms you'll meet:

- **Matricule** — your unique student number, issued only after you are admitted. It looks like `stm-2026-0001`. You can use it to sign in once you become a student.
- **Programme** — the degree path you are applying for (for example HND, Bachelors, or Masters).
- **Level** — the year/stage you are entering within a programme.

## Getting in

### 1. Create your account

1. Open SchuLyf in your browser. You land on the **Log in** screen.
2. Under the form, select **Sign up** (next to "Don't have an account?"). You arrive at the **Create your account** page.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — your full name.
   - **Email address** — a real address you can check; this is where SchuLyf sends important messages.
   - **Phone number (optional)** — you can leave this blank.
   - **Password** and **Confirm password** — type the same password twice. Use the eye icon to reveal what you typed.
4. Select **Create account**.

If the email is already in use, you'll see an error under the **Email address** field. If you have applied or studied here before and your account was closed, do **not** create a new account — see [I had an account before](#i-had-an-account-here-before-but-i-cant-sign-in) below.

### 2. Verify your email

Before you can do anything else, you must confirm your email address.

1. After registering you see the **Verify email** screen: *"Please verify your email address by clicking on the link we just emailed to you."*
2. Open your inbox and click the verification link in the email from SchuLyf.
3. If the email hasn't arrived, return to this screen and select **Resend verification email**. You'll see a confirmation: *"A new verification link has been sent…"*

Until your email is verified, the application form and your dashboard stay locked. You can select **Log out** from this screen at any time.

### 3. Sign in

1. From the **Log in** ("Welcome back") page, in the **Username** field, enter the **email address** you registered with. (The small info icon notes that returning users may also use an employee ID or matricule — as a new applicant, use your email.)
2. Enter your **Password**.
3. Optionally tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device.
4. Select **Log in**.

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** and follow [Reset your password](#reset-your-password).

## What you can do

Once signed in and verified, you land on your **Applicant Dashboard**. From here you can:

- **Start a new application** for admission to a programme.
- **Track all your applications** in one list, each with its current status.
- **Open any application** to see its full detail, the documents you submitted, and any decision notes.
- **Manage your account** — update your name, email and phone; change your password; switch between light and dark appearance.

### Your dashboard at a glance

The dashboard greets you with **"Welcome back"** and your first name. Below that:

- A **New application** button (top right) to begin applying.
- Four summary chips: **Submitted**, **In review**, **Admitted**, and **Rejected**, counting your applications by status.
- A **My applications** table listing each application's **Department**, **Programme**, **Level**, **Status**, **Submitted** date, and a **View** link.

If you haven't applied yet, the table shows *"You haven't submitted any applications yet"* with a **Start your first application** button.

## Task walkthroughs

### Submit an admission application

The application form is a guided, 4-step wizard: **Programme → Personal → Documents → Review**. You must complete the steps in order.

1. On your dashboard, select **New application** (or **Start your first application**). You arrive at the **New application** page.

2. **Step 1 — Programme**
   - **Degree programme** — choose your programme (for example HND, Bachelors, Masters).
   - **Department** — once you've picked a programme, this list fills with the departments that offer it. Choose one.
   - **Level** — pick the level you're entering. The form shows the allowed range (for example "Allowed: 1 – 3") and won't let you go outside it.
   - Select **Continue**.
   - *If the departments don't load*, you'll see a red message — check your internet connection and select **Retry**.

3. **Step 2 — Personal details**
   - Enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Contact email**, **Phone**, and **Date of birth** (pick it from the calendar).
   - **Previous institute** is optional.
   - Select **Continue** (or **Back** to change your programme).
   - Note: the **Contact email** here is the address where your admission decision will be sent. It can differ from your sign-in email, so make sure it's correct.

4. **Step 3 — Required documents**
   - Two documents — **proof of identity** and your **birth certificate** — are required for every application. Depending on your programme and level, more may be listed.
   - Each document has its own upload box. Select **Choose file** and pick the file. Accepted formats are **PDF, JPG or PNG**, up to **8 MB** each.
   - When a file is attached, its name appears under the document. If you picked the wrong one, clear it and choose again.
   - If you haven't selected a programme and level yet, you'll see a note asking you to do so first.
   - Select **Continue** (or **Back**).

5. **Step 4 — Review & submit**
   - Check the summary cards: **Programme**, **Personal details**, and **Documents** (each document shows the file name, or "Not uploaded").
   - To fix anything, select **Back** and edit the relevant step.
   - When everything is correct, select **Submit application**.

6. **How to tell it worked** — you're returned to your dashboard, a green **"Application submitted."** message appears, and your new application shows in **My applications** with the status **Submitted**.

**Common problems when submitting**

- **A field is highlighted in red after you submit.** The form jumps back to the step that has the problem and shows a message under the field. Fix it and submit again.
- **A document is rejected.** Make sure the file is a PDF, JPG, or PNG and under 8 MB, and that every required document slot has a file. Re-upload and submit again.
- **"You already have an application in progress."** You can only have **one open application at a time**. Wait for a decision on your current application before starting another. (Applications that have been decided — admitted, rejected, etc. — no longer count as open.)

### Check the status of an application

1. On your dashboard, find the application in **My applications**.
2. Select **View** on its row. You arrive at the application detail page (titled **Application #** followed by its number).
3. At the top you'll see a coloured status tag, the **Department**, **Programme · Level**, your details, the **Submitted at** date, and — once a decision is made — the **Decided at** date and any **Decision notes** from the admissions office.
4. The **Submitted documents** section lists each file you uploaded, with its type, filename, size, and upload time.

**What each status means**

| Status | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Your application has been received and is waiting to be looked at. |
| **Under review** | The admissions office is actively reviewing your application. |
| **Documents requested** | The office rejected one or more of your documents and needs you to re-upload them. Open the application to see which documents were rejected and why, then replace them — see [Respond to a documents request](#respond-to-a-documents-request). |
| **Admitted** | Congratulations — you've been accepted. See [What happens when you're admitted](#what-happens-when-youre-admitted). |
| **Rejected** | Your application was not accepted. The **Decision notes** may explain why. |
| **Waitlisted** | You haven't been accepted yet but may be offered a place later. |
| **Withdrawn** | The application was closed — for example, because a returning student's previous enrolment was restored instead. |

You don't need to do anything to advance a status — the admissions office moves it. You'll be emailed once a final decision is made (see [Notifications](#notifications)).

### Respond to a documents request

If the admissions office needs better copies of some documents, your application moves to **Documents requested** and you receive an email listing what to replace. Here's how to fix it:

1. Open the application from your dashboard (**View** on its row). At the top you'll see a yellow banner telling you some documents need attention.
2. Scroll to the documents list. Each document now shows a **Status**:
   - **Accepted** — this one is fine, nothing to do.
   - **Awaiting review** — not looked at yet.
   - **Rejected** — needs replacing. The reason the office gave is shown in red underneath.
3. For each **Rejected** document, select **Replace**, then choose the corrected file. Accepted formats are **PDF, JPG, or PNG**, up to **8 MB**. Only rejected documents can be replaced.
4. The file uploads immediately and that document returns to **Awaiting review**. A confirmation message tells you it will be re-checked.

**How to tell it worked** — once you've replaced the **last** rejected document, your application automatically returns to **Submitted** and goes back into the office's queue — you don't resubmit anything. If some documents are still rejected, the application stays in **Documents requested** until you've replaced them all.

> You won't get a second email each time you replace a single document. The office emails you once per request; you just keep replacing the rejected documents until none remain.

### What happens when you're admitted

When your application status becomes **Admitted**:

- You officially become a **student**, and SchuLyf issues your **matricule** (your student number, like `stm-2026-0001`). Your admission email includes this number.
- Your account gains student access. After you sign in again, you'll land on the **student** area instead of the applicant dashboard.
- From there you can sign in using your email **or** your new matricule.

For everything you can do as a student — paying tuition, viewing receipts, results, courses and more — see the **[Student guide](student.md)**.

### Update your profile

1. Open **Settings → Profile** (Profile settings).
2. Edit your **Name**, **Email address**, or **Phone number (optional)**.
3. Select **Save**.

If you change your email and it isn't verified, the page shows *"Your email address is unverified"* with a link to **resend the verification email** — use it and click the link in your inbox.

### Change your password

1. Open **Settings → Security** (Security settings), under **Update password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**, then your **New password**, and **Confirm password**.
3. Select **Save password**.

### Reset your password

If you can't sign in:

1. On the **Log in** page, select **Forgot password?**
2. On the **Forgot password** page, enter your **Email address** and select **Email password reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email you receive.
4. On the **Reset password** page (your email is shown and locked), enter a new **Password** and **Confirm password**, then select **Reset password**.
5. Sign in with your new password.

### Switch between light and dark mode

1. Open **Settings → Appearance** (Appearance settings).
2. Choose your preferred appearance. The change applies immediately.

### Verify a school receipt (optional)

SchuLyf has a **public** receipt-verification page anyone can use to confirm a school payment receipt is genuine — you don't even need to sign in. You won't normally use this as an applicant, but it's there if someone hands you a receipt to check.

Open the receipt-verification link for the receipt number you have:

- A genuine receipt shows a green **Authentic receipt** banner with the student's name, matricule, programme, academic year, amount, and issue date. Confirm those details match the person presenting it.
- A bad code shows a red **Invalid receipt** banner: *"We could not verify this receipt… Do not accept it as proof of payment — contact the accounts office."*

## Notifications

- **Email verification** — sent when you register (and whenever you select **Resend verification email**). You must click its link before you can use the app.
- **Password reset** — sent when you request one from **Forgot password?**.
- **Documents requested** — if the office rejects one or more of your documents, SchuLyf emails you a list of exactly which documents to replace, with a link to your application. See [Respond to a documents request](#respond-to-a-documents-request). You get one email each time the office makes a request.
- **Admission decision** — once the admissions office reaches a **final** decision (admitted, rejected, or waitlisted, or a prior enrolment being restored), SchuLyf emails you. The email goes to the **Contact email** you entered on the application form, not necessarily your sign-in email. If you're admitted, the email includes your new **matricule**.

Both the documents-request and decision emails go to the **Contact email** on your application form. You are **not** emailed for other in-progress steps like "under review". To see where things stand at any time, open the application from your dashboard.

If a decision email doesn't arrive, check the spam folder and confirm the **Contact email** on your application is correct (open the application's detail page to see it).

## FAQ / Troubleshooting

**I registered but can't reach the dashboard or the application form.**
You haven't verified your email yet. Open the email from SchuLyf and click the link, or select **Resend verification email** on the **Verify email** screen.

**The verification email never arrived.**
On the **Verify email** screen select **Resend verification email**, then check your inbox and spam folder. Make sure the email address you registered with is spelled correctly.

**I can only have one application — can I apply for two programmes at once?**
No. You may have just **one open application** at a time. Wait for it to be decided before starting another.

**My document won't upload.**
Files must be **PDF, JPG, or PNG** and **8 MB or smaller**. Convert or compress larger files, and make sure every required document slot has a file before you submit.

**The "Continue" button won't move me forward / a field is red.**
Something on that step needs fixing. Complete every required field (programme, department, level, your personal details, and all required documents). After a failed submit the form jumps to the step with the problem and shows a message under the field.

**The department or document lists won't load.**
This is usually a connection issue. Use the **Retry** button shown in the red message, or refresh the page and try again.

**I had an account here before but I can't sign in.**
If your account was closed, you cannot simply register again with the same email — that will be refused. Instead, reactivate it through **Forgot password?**: request a reset link, set a new password, and you'll be signed back in. You'll start on the applicant dashboard and can apply again; the admissions office links you back to your previous enrolment during review. (Staff accounts are restored by an administrator, not this way.)

**Where do I find my student number (matricule)?**
You receive it only when you're **admitted** — it's in your admission email and used for student sign-in afterwards.

**I was admitted — what now?**
You're a student. Sign in again to reach the student area, and follow the **[Student guide](student.md)**.
