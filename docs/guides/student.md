# Student guide

*Your everyday guide to SchuLyf — the Student Management System.*

---

## Who this guide is for

This guide is for **admitted students**. Once your application has been accepted and you have been
enrolled, your account becomes a *student* account. From it you can:

- See your enrollment details (your matricule, programme, level and academic year).
- Report tuition deposits you have made at the bank, and track when the accounts office validates them.
- View and print your **official school receipt** once a payment is validated.
- Check whether you are *cleared* to sit exams and use campus facilities — and ask for more time if
  you are short on tuition.
- See the courses you'll be taking each semester.
- Follow your course attendance.
- Submit assignments and see your grades and feedback.
- Read your CA and exam results, and raise a dispute if a score looks wrong.
- Get notified when a lecturer cancels or reschedules a class.

A few terms used throughout:

- **Matricule** — your unique student registration number. It identifies you on every receipt and
  record.
- **CA (continuous assessment)** — the marks you earn during the term (assignments, tests), separate
  from your end-of-term **exam** mark.
- **Deferral** — written permission from the accounts office to keep your exam and facility access for
  a longer period while you finish paying tuition.
- **XAF** — Central African CFA franc, the currency tuition is shown in.

## Getting in

### How you get a student account

You don't create a student account yourself. It starts as an **applicant** account (which you
register and verify by email when you apply). When the student affairs office admits your application
and enrols you, that same account is upgraded to a **student** account — same email, same password.

So:

1. Sign in with the email and password you used to apply.
2. After admission, signing in takes you straight to your **Student Dashboard**.

### Signing in

1. Go to the app's web address. You will land on the **sign-in** page.
2. Enter your **email** and **password** and sign in.
3. If your account has two-factor authentication turned on, you'll also be asked for the one-time
   code from your authenticator app.

> **Email verification.** Your email address must be verified to use the app. If you see a banner
> saying *"Your email address is unverified,"* open **Settings → Profile** and use
> **"Click here to resend the verification email,"** then click the link in the email.

If you can't get in, see **FAQ / troubleshooting** at the end.

## What you can do — your dashboard at a glance

After signing in you land on the **Student Dashboard** (the welcome screen). It has:

- A greeting (**"Welcome back, …"**) and a coloured status tag showing your enrollment status
  (for example **Active**).
- **Quick links** — five shortcut tiles: **My payments**, **My courses**, **My attendance**,
  **My assignments**, **My results**. Click any tile to jump to that section.
- **Notifications** — your latest in-app messages (for example, a cancelled or rescheduled class),
  with an unread count and a **Mark all read** button.
- **My enrollment** — your **Matricule**, **Programme**, **Level** and **Academic year**.
- **Application history** — the applications on your record, each with a **View** button.

If your enrollment area says *"No enrollment record found,"* contact the student affairs office.

You can reach the same sections any time from the navigation menu or the dashboard quick links.

## Task walkthroughs

### 1. Report a tuition deposit and upload your bank slip

You pay tuition by depositing money into one of the institute's bank accounts, keeping the bank's
deposit slip, and then **reporting** that deposit here. The accounts office checks it and issues your
official school receipt. Uploading the slip means a lost paper copy no longer stops you.

**Where:** Quick link **My payments** (the **Tuition payments** page).

At the top of this page you'll see your matricule, programme and academic year, and — if a fee
schedule has been set up for you — a summary box with:

- **Tuition total** — what you owe for the year.
- **Validated paid** — how much of your reported payments the accounts office has confirmed.
- **Remaining** — what's left.

> If you see *"No fee schedule has been configured for your enrollment yet,"* you can still report a
> deposit; the accounts office will validate it.

**To report a deposit:**

1. Scroll to the **Report a deposit** box.
2. **Bank** — choose the bank where you made the deposit (UBA, AFG, AFRICLAND, SCB or SGC).
3. **Amount (XAF)** — type the amount you deposited.
4. **Bank transaction reference** — enter the reference number printed on your bank slip.
5. **Bank slip** — click **Choose file** and pick a photo or scan of your slip.
   *Accepted formats: PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 8 MB.*
6. Click **Report payment**.

**How to tell it worked:** the form clears and a new row appears in the **My reported deposits**
table below, with a **Submitted** status tag. *Submitted* means "received, waiting for the accounts
office to check it" — it is not yet confirmed.

**Common problems and what to do:**

- **A field is highlighted in red with a message** — a required field is missing or invalid. Fix the
  highlighted field (for example choose a bank, or pick a smaller slip file) and click **Report
  payment** again.
- **"No active student enrollment found"** appears instead of the form — there's nothing to pay
  against yet. Contact the student affairs office.

### 2. Check the status of a reported payment

**Where:** **My payments** → the **My reported deposits** table.

Each deposit shows a **Status** tag:

- **Submitted** — received, awaiting review by the accounts office.
- **Validated** — confirmed. The amount now counts toward your **Validated paid** total, and a
  **View** link appears in the **Receipt** column.
- **Rejected** — not accepted. The reason appears in the **Note** column.

Useful columns in the same table:

- **Slip** — a download icon to retrieve the bank slip you uploaded.
- **Receipt** — a **View** link once the payment is validated (see the next task).
- **Note** — the rejection reason, if any.

**If a deposit is rejected:** read the reason in the **Note** column, then report the deposit again
correctly (for example with the right reference number or a clearer slip image) using the **Report a
deposit** form. You'll also receive an email about the decision.

### 3. View and print your official school receipt

Once a payment is **Validated**, the accounts office issues your **Official school receipt** — your
single, tamper-proof proof of payment.

**Where:** **My payments** → in the **Receipt** column of a validated deposit, click **View**.

The receipt page shows your name, **matricule**, programme and level, academic year, the bank and
reference, the date issued, and the **Amount validated**.

- Click **Print** (top right) to print it or save it as a PDF.
- Click **Back** to return to your payments page.

**About the verification code / QR.** Every receipt carries a **QR code** and a verification link,
marked **Tamper-proof**. Anyone — a staff member at an exam hall, for example — can scan the code or
open the link to confirm the receipt is genuine and belongs to you. Because the receipt is tied to
your identity, it cannot be edited, forged or used by another student without the check failing. You
never need to do anything to "activate" this; it's built in.

> More background: see the [Payments & school receipts module](../modules/payments.md).

### 4. Understand your payment standing and exam access

During the exam period, only students who have paid enough tuition are allowed into the exam hall and
certain facilities. The **My payments** page shows your current **standing** as a coloured tag with a
short explanation:

- **Cleared** — *"You are cleared for exams and facilities."* Nothing to do.
- **Blocked** — you are behind on a tuition installment whose deadline has passed. The page tells you
  how much to **Pay … to clear, or request a deferral.**
- **Deferred** — you were short, but the accounts office granted you extra time. The page shows the
  date your access is extended to and the amount to settle before then.

Tuition can be paid in installments, each with its own amount and deadline. Once a deadline passes,
if your **validated** payments are below the amount due by then, your standing becomes **Blocked** —
until you either pay the shortfall or are granted a deferral.

> Your standing is worked out from confirmed (validated) payments only. A deposit still marked
> **Submitted** does not count yet, so allow time for the accounts office to validate it before a
> deadline. Details: [Payment standing & deferrals module](../modules/exam-gating.md).

### 5. Request a tuition deferral (extra time to pay)

If your standing is **Blocked** (or **Deferred** but you need more time), you can ask the accounts
office to extend your access deadline.

**Where:** **My payments** → the standing box → **Request deferral** button (shown when you are not
already cleared).

1. Click **Request deferral**. A **Request a tuition deferral** window opens.
2. **Reason** — explain why you need more time (required).
3. **Desired new deadline (optional)** — you may suggest a date. The accounts office decides the
   actual date.
4. Click **Submit request**.

**How to tell it worked:** the window closes and a **My deferral requests** section appears (or
updates) on the page, showing your request with a **Requested** status tag.

Each request shows a status:

- **Requested** — submitted, awaiting a decision.
- **Approved** — granted. The **Extended to** column shows your new deadline, and your standing
  becomes **Deferred**.
- **Rejected** — declined. Any explanation appears in the **Note** column.

**Common problems and what to do:**

- **The Request deferral button is greyed out** — you already have a request awaiting a decision. You
  can submit a new one only after the current one is approved or rejected.
- **A message says you already have an open request, or asks for a reason** — you can have only one
  open request at a time per academic year, and the reason cannot be empty. Wait for the existing
  request's decision, or add a reason.

**If your deferral is denied (Rejected):** the fastest way to restore access is to pay the shortfall
shown in your standing box. You'll be emailed about the decision, and you may submit a fresh deferral
request afterwards if your situation has changed.

### 6. See the courses you'll be taking each semester

**Where:** Quick link **My courses** (the **My courses** page).

At the top you'll see your programme, level and academic year. Below, your courses are grouped
into a card per **semester** (with the course count and total credits for that semester). Each
course row shows:

- **Code** and **Course** — the course code, title and a short description.
- **Credits** — the course's credit value.
- **Lecturer** — who teaches it (or *"Not yet assigned"*).
- **Sessions** and **Assignments** — how many class sessions and assignments the course has so far.

Only courses whose plan has been **approved** for your programme, level and academic year appear
here. If the page says no courses have been published yet, your department hasn't finalised the
course plans — check back later.

### 7. View your course attendance

**Where:** Quick link **My attendance** (the **My attendance** page).

You'll see a card per course you're enrolled in. Each card lists the course's **sessions** with:

- **Scheduled** — the session's date and time.
- **Topic** — what the session covers.
- **Session** — the session's own status: **Scheduled**, **Held** or **Cancelled**.
- **My attendance** — how you were marked: **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excused** (a dash
  means it hasn't been recorded).

If you aren't enrolled in any courses yet, the page says so.

> Attendance is recorded by your lecturers; this page is read-only. See the
> [Course management module](../modules/course-management.md).

### 8. Submit an assignment

**Where:** Quick link **My assignments** (the **My assignments** page).

You'll see a card per course, listing each assignment with its **Title**, **Instructions**, **Due**
date/time, and a **Your submission** column.

**To submit:**

1. Find the assignment and click **Submit** in the **Your submission** column.
2. In the **Submit assignment** window, click **Choose file** and pick your file.
   *Accepted: PDF, JPG or PNG — max 8 MB.*
3. Click **Submit**.

**How to tell it worked:** the window closes and your assignment now shows a **Submitted** tag. If
you submitted after the due date, a **Late** tag also appears.

**To view or replace your submission:** use **View** to preview the file (with options to open it in
a new tab or download it), or **Replace** to upload a different file before grading.

**After grading:** the tag changes to **Graded** and your score appears as *score / maximum* (for
example **14/20**), with any **feedback** from the lecturer beneath it.

**Common problems and what to do:**

- **The Submit button stays disabled** — you haven't chosen a file yet. Pick a file first.
- **A red message about the file** — the file is the wrong type or too large. Use a PDF, JPG or PNG
  under 8 MB.

### 9. View your CA and exam results

**Where:** Quick link **My results** (the **My results** page).

Results appear here once they have been **published**. You'll see a card per course showing:

- **CA** — your continuous-assessment mark.
- **Exam** — your exam mark.
- **Final** — your combined final score.
- **Grade** — your letter grade.

A dash (—) means that figure isn't available. If nothing has been published yet, the page tells you
your results will appear here once published.

### 10. Raise a dispute on a result

If a published score looks wrong, you can flag it for review.

**Where:** **My results** → the course card → **Raise a dispute**.

1. Click **Raise a dispute**. A window opens.
2. **Reason** — explain what you think is wrong (required).
3. Click **Submit dispute**.

**How to tell it worked:** the window closes and the course card now shows **Your dispute** with an
**Open** status tag and the reason you wrote.

Your dispute status can be:

- **Open** — submitted, not yet looked at.
- **Under review** — being checked.
- **Resolved** — a decision has been made; any explanation shows beneath your dispute.
- **Rejected** — declined; any explanation shows beneath your dispute.

**Common problem:** if you see *"You already have an open dispute for this result,"* you can only have
one active dispute per result. Wait for the current one to be resolved or rejected before raising
another.

## Notifications

SchuLyf reaches you two ways.

**In-app (on your dashboard).** When a lecturer **cancels** or **reschedules** one of your upcoming
classes, a message appears in the **Notifications** card on your dashboard, showing the course, the
date/time, and any reason given. The most recent messages are listed there.

- Click a message (or its **Mark read** button) to mark it read.
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread count for everything at once.

**Email.** Some outcomes are emailed to the address on your account:

- When a tuition payment you reported is **validated** or **rejected**.
- When a deferral request is **approved** or **rejected**.
- (Cancelled/rescheduled classes are emailed too, in addition to the in-app message.)

> Keep your email address current under **Settings → Profile** so you don't miss these. More on how
> notifications work: [Notifications module](../modules/notifications.md).

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the menu. It has three areas:

- **Profile** — update your **Name**, **Email address** and optional **Phone number**, then click
  **Save**. If your email is unverified, the verification prompt appears here. This page also has a
  **Delete account** option.
- **Security** — change your password (enter your **Current password**, then a **New password** and
  confirm it, and click **Save password**). If two-factor authentication is available, you can enable
  or disable it and manage your recovery codes here.
- **Appearance** — switch the app's look (for example light or dark mode).

## FAQ / troubleshooting

**I can't sign in.**
Check your email and password. If you've forgotten your password, use the password-reset link on the
sign-in page. If a banner says your email is unverified, verify it from **Settings → Profile**.

**My dashboard says "No enrollment record found."**
Your account isn't linked to an active enrollment. Contact the student affairs office.

**I lost my bank deposit slip.**
As long as you already **reported** the deposit and uploaded the slip here, your record is safe — the
**Slip** column on **My payments** lets you download the copy you uploaded. If you never uploaded it
and can't re-obtain it from the bank, contact the accounts office with your bank transaction
reference.

**My reported payment still shows "Submitted."**
It's waiting for the accounts office to check it. You'll get an email when it's validated or rejected,
and the status here will update. Only **Validated** payments count toward your standing, so report
deposits well before an installment deadline.

**My payment was rejected.**
Open **My payments**, read the reason in the **Note** column, and report the deposit again with the
correction (right reference number, clearer slip, correct amount).

**I'm Blocked and exams are near.**
Pay the shortfall shown in your standing box (it counts once the accounts office validates it), or
click **Request deferral** to ask for more time. If a deferral is granted, your standing becomes
**Deferred** with an extended deadline.

**My deferral was denied.**
Pay the shortfall to clear, or submit a new deferral request if your circumstances have changed.

**I can't upload my slip or assignment file.**
The file must be a PDF, JPG or PNG and under 8 MB. Convert or compress the file and try again.

**I think a grade is wrong.**
On **My results**, click **Raise a dispute**, give your reason, and submit. You can have one open
dispute per result at a time.

**A class shows as cancelled/rescheduled — is it real?**
Yes. Cancellation and reschedule notices come from your lecturer through the app (and email), so the
notice in your dashboard's **Notifications** card is the trustworthy source.
