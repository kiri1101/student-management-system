# Accountant guide

This guide is for **accountants and finance staff** at the accounts office. It walks you through everything you do in SchuLyf, the school's online student-management system: confirming that students have actually paid their tuition, issuing each paid student their proof of payment, deciding requests for more time to pay, and checking on the spot whether a student is cleared to sit exams.

You do **not** need any technical background to use this guide. Every step below matches a button or field you will see on screen. Where a less-familiar word appears (like *matricule* or *deferral*), it is explained the first time it is used.

---

## Who this guide is for

You are an **accountant**. In SchuLyf you can:

- Work through the **payment queue**: open each student's reported bank deposit, check the bank slip they uploaded, and either **validate** it (which issues their official school receipt) or **reject** it.
- Work through the **deferral queue**: decide requests from students who are short on tuition and are asking for more time before they lose access to exams and facilities.
- Run a **Standing check**: type in a student's matricule and instantly see whether they are cleared for exams.
- Update your own staff profile and account settings.

A few terms you will meet throughout:

- **Matricule** — a student's unique enrollment number (for example `stm-2026-0001`). It identifies one student.
- **Bank slip** — the deposit receipt the bank gives a student when they pay tuition into one of the school's bank accounts (UBA, AFG, AFRICLAND, SCB, or SGC). The student uploads a photo or PDF of this slip when reporting their payment.
- **School receipt** — the school's own official proof of payment, issued by you when you validate a payment. It is tamper-proof and tied to one student, so it cannot be faked, edited, or reused by someone else.
- **Deferral** — formal permission for a student who has not yet paid enough to keep their exam and facility access for a little longer, up to a new deadline you set.
- **Standing** — a student's current payment position for exam access: **Cleared**, **Blocked**, or **Deferred**.

## Getting in

### Your account

Accountant accounts are **created for you by an administrator** — you do not sign yourself up. When your account is set up, you receive an **invitation link** by email. Open that link and follow the prompts to set your own password. This is a one-time step.

### Signing in

1. Go to the SchuLyf sign-in page. (Visiting the site address takes you straight to it.)
2. In the **Username** field, type any one of the following — they all work:
   - your work **email address**, or
   - your **employee ID** (for example `EMP-001`).
   The small information icon next to the field confirms this: *"Email, employee ID, or Matricule."*
3. Enter your **Password** (you can tap the eye icon to reveal what you typed).
4. Optionally tick **Remember me** so you stay signed in on this device.
5. Click **Log in**.

You land on your **Accountant Dashboard**.

> **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email link to set a new one. The same flow works if you ever need to regain access.

> **Email verification.** Some screens require your email address to be verified. If you are ever blocked from a page for this reason, open **Settings → Profile**; if a notice says your email is unverified, click the resend link, then open the verification email and click its link.

## What you can do — the dashboard tour

When you sign in you see the **Accountant Dashboard**:

- A **Welcome back** banner with a **Review payments** button (your quickest route into the payment queue).
- Four count chips across the top:
  - **Submitted** — payments waiting for you to review.
  - **Validated** — payments you (or a colleague) have already approved.
  - **Rejected** — payments that were turned down.
  - **Deferrals requested** — deferral requests waiting for a decision.
- **My profile** — shows your **Bank desk** and **Cashier window** (whatever was recorded for you; a dash `—` means it is blank). If you see "No accountant profile found," contact an administrator.
- **Deferral requests** — a running tally of deferral requests by outcome (Requested / Approved / Rejected), with a **Review deferrals** button.

The left-hand menu lets you jump to **Payments**, **Deferrals**, the **Standing check**, and your **Settings** at any time.

## Task walkthroughs

### 1. Validate a student's payment (and issue their school receipt)

This is the core of your day. When a student reports that they deposited tuition, it lands in your payment queue as **Submitted**. Your job is to compare what they reported against the bank slip they uploaded, then validate or reject it.

**Open the queue**

1. From the dashboard, click **Review payments** (or choose **Payments** in the left menu).
2. The **Payments** page opens. By default it shows the payments that need action — those with status **Submitted**.
3. Each row shows the **Student** (name and matricule), the **Bank** and the bank reference, the **Amount**, the **Year**, the **Status**, and when it was **Reported**. You can sort by **Amount** or **Reported** by clicking those column headers.
4. To see other payments (for example ones you already validated or rejected), use the **Filter by status** box at the top right and pick the statuses you want.

**Review one payment**

5. Click **Review** at the end of the row. The **Payment review** page opens, showing the student, their programme, the bank, the bank reference, the amount, the academic year, when it was reported, and the **Bank slip**.
6. Check the bank slip:
   - Click **View slip** to open it **right in the browser** — no download needed. A viewer window shows the uploaded slip (PDF or image).
   - Or click the **filename link** next to it to download the slip.
7. Confirm the slip is genuine and that the **amount** and **bank reference** on the slip match what the student reported. (The on-screen hint says exactly this: *"Verify the bank slip against the reported amount and reference, then validate or reject this payment."*)

**If everything checks out — validate**

8. Click **Validate**.
9. You are returned to the queue and a confirmation appears: **"Payment validated."**
10. The payment's status becomes **Validated**, and SchuLyf automatically issues the student their **school receipt** — the tamper-proof, student-specific proof of payment. The student is emailed that their payment was validated and can then print their receipt themselves. You do not need to hand over any paper.

> **How to tell it worked:** the payment now shows **Validated**, and if you reopen it, a **Review outcome** panel shows your name under **Reviewed by** and the time under **Reviewed at**.

**If something is wrong — reject**

8. Click **Reject**.
9. A **Reject payment** box appears. Type a clear **Reason** (for example, "Bank slip amount does not match the reported amount" or "Slip is unreadable"). A reason is **required** — if you leave it blank you'll be asked to fill it in.
10. Click **Reject payment** to confirm (or **Cancel** to back out).
11. You return to the queue with the confirmation **"Payment rejected."** The student is emailed the outcome and can see the reason you gave, so they can fix the issue and report the deposit again.

> **A payment can only be decided once.** Once a payment is **Validated** or **Rejected**, the Validate/Reject buttons no longer appear — you instead see the **Review outcome** panel with who decided it, when, and (for rejections) the reason. If two people open the same payment at once, only the first decision is accepted; the second will be told it has already been reviewed.

**Common situations**

- **The student lost their paper bank slip.** They don't need it on paper — what matters is the slip they uploaded when reporting the deposit, which you view on the review page. There is no paper to chase.
- **The slip won't open.** If **View slip** shows nothing usable, download it via the filename link. If it is still unreadable, reject the payment with a reason asking for a clearer slip.

### 2. Decide a tuition deferral request

A student who has not paid enough by an installment deadline normally loses access to exams and facilities. They can ask the accounts office for more time — a **deferral**. Those requests come to you.

**Open the deferral queue**

1. From the dashboard, click **Review deferrals** (or choose **Deferrals** in the left menu).
2. The **Deferrals** page lists requests that need action first. Each row shows the **Student** (name and matricule), the **Reason** they gave, the **Year**, their **Desired deadline** (the date the student is hoping for — advisory only), and the **Status**. Use **Filter by status** to see approved or rejected ones too.

**Review one request**

3. Click **Review** on the row. The **Deferral review** page shows the student, their programme, the academic year, the **Desired deadline**, and the full **Reason**.

> Tip: before deciding, you can run a **Standing check** (see Task 4) on that student's matricule to see exactly how much they still owe.

**To grant the request — approve**

4. Click **Approve**.
5. An **Approve deferral** box appears. Set the **Extended deadline** — the date until which the student keeps their exam and facility access. This date is **required** and must be in the future. (You are not bound by the student's desired date; you set the binding one.)
6. Optionally add **Notes**.
7. Click **Approve deferral** (or **Cancel**).
8. You return to the queue. From now until the extended deadline, that student's standing shows as **Deferred** rather than **Blocked**, so they are cleared for exams. The student is emailed that their request was approved.

**To turn it down — reject**

4. Click **Reject**.
5. A **Reject deferral** box appears. Type a **Reason**; this is **required**.
6. Click **Reject deferral** (or **Cancel**).
7. You return to the queue, and the student is emailed that the request was rejected, along with your reason.

> **How to tell it worked:** the request's status updates to **Approved** or **Rejected**, and reopening it shows a **Review outcome** panel with who decided it, the **Extended deadline** (for approvals), and any notes.

> **A deferral can only be decided once,** just like payments. After a decision, the Approve/Reject buttons disappear. An approved deferral only keeps a student cleared **until the extended deadline you set** — once that date passes, if they still owe, they go back to being blocked.

---

### 3. Print or re-issue a receipt

You do not issue paper receipts. When you **validate** a payment (Task 1), SchuLyf issues the student's official school receipt automatically and notifies them. The **student** prints their own receipt from their account, and anyone can confirm a receipt is genuine using its receipt number. If a student asks you to "give them the receipt," point them to their own payments page — validating the payment is all the action required from you.

### 4. Check a student's standing (exam eligibility)

Use the **Standing check** when someone (a student at your desk, an exam invigilator, a colleague) needs to know on the spot whether a student is cleared to sit exams and use facilities.

1. Choose **Standing check** from the left menu.
2. In the **Matricule** field, type the student's matricule (for example `stm-2026-0001`).
3. Click **Check**.

**Reading the result**

If the matricule exists, a card shows the student's name with a **standing tag**:

- **Cleared** (green) — the student is up to date (or nothing is due yet) and may sit exams.
- **Blocked** (red) — the student owes money past a deadline and is **not** cleared. They would need to pay or be granted a deferral.
- **Deferred** (amber) — the student owes money but has an approved deferral that is still in effect, so they remain cleared until the deferral's deadline.

Below that you see the student's **Matricule**, **Programme** and level, and **Academic year**. If a fee schedule applies, a panel breaks down the figures:

- **Due so far** — how much should have been paid by today.
- **Validated paid** — how much of their paid tuition you (or a colleague) have validated. *Only validated payments count* — a payment still sitting as Submitted does not.
- **Shortfall** — the gap they still need to cover.
- **Deferred until** — shown only when a deferral is active; the date their extra time runs out.

**Other messages**

- **"No student found for that matricule."** — the matricule was mistyped or doesn't exist. Re-check it and search again.
- **"No fee schedule is configured for this enrollment, so nothing is owed yet."** — no fees have been set up for that student's programme/level/year yet, so they are treated as cleared.

> This lookup is staff-only and shows who is behind on fees, so use it discreetly.

---

### 5. Manage your settings

Open **Settings** from the left menu. You'll find:

- **Profile** — update your **Name**, **Email address**, and optional **Phone number**, then click **Save**. If your email is unverified, a notice here lets you resend the verification email.
- **Staff profile** — under your **Accountant profile**, set your **Bank desk** and **Cashier window**, then click **Save**. (Your name and email live on the Profile tab, not here.) These are the details shown on your dashboard's **My profile** card.
- **Security** — change your password (enter your **Current password**, then a new one twice, and click **Save password**). If two-factor authentication is available to you, you can enable it here for an extra sign-in code.
- **Appearance** — switch between light and dark display.

## Notifications

SchuLyf keeps students informed by **email** on your behalf — you don't have to send anything:

- When you **validate or reject a payment**, the student is automatically emailed the outcome (and, for rejections, the reason you gave).
- When you **approve or reject a deferral**, the student is automatically emailed the decision (and, for rejections, your reason).

On your own side, your **dashboard chips** are your live worklist: **Submitted** tells you how many payments are waiting, and **Deferrals requested** tells you how many deferral decisions are pending. Watch those counts to know when there's work to do.

## FAQ / troubleshooting

**A student says they paid but I don't see their payment.**
You only review payments students have **reported**. If nothing appears, the student hasn't yet submitted their deposit (with the uploaded slip) on their own payments page. Ask them to report it first; it will then show as **Submitted** in your queue.

**The student lost their bank slip — can I still validate?**
You work from the slip they **uploaded** when reporting the deposit, viewable on the review page. The paper copy isn't needed. If they never uploaded a usable slip, the payment can't be validated; reject it asking for a clear slip, and they can resubmit.

**I validated the wrong payment / rejected one by mistake.**
A decision is final from the screen — the Validate/Reject buttons disappear once a payment is decided, and the school receipt for a validated payment cannot be changed. Contact an administrator to sort out a mistake.

**A student is at the exam hall and says they're blocked unfairly.**
Run a **Standing check** on their matricule (Task 4). It shows **Due so far**, **Validated paid**, and **Shortfall**, which explains exactly why they are Blocked. If a payment of theirs is still **Submitted**, validating it may clear them. If they genuinely need more time, they can request a deferral for you to approve.

**The deferral box won't let me approve.**
The **Extended deadline** is required and must be a **future** date. Pick a valid date and try again. For a rejection, the **Reason** is required.

**My dashboard says "No accountant profile found."**
Your staff profile hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator; this doesn't stop you reviewing payments or deferrals, but the **Bank desk** / **Cashier window** details won't show.

**I can't reach a page — it asks me to verify my email.**
Open **Settings → Profile**, resend the verification email if prompted, then click the link in that email. Reload the page afterwards.
